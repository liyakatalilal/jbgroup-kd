Executive Overview

A Legacy Built on
Excellence in Service

We are an established hospitality management group with operations across India and Iraq, now formally presenting our credentials to the Sultanate of Oman in the hope of contributing to the nation's remarkable Vision 2040 journey.

Our portfolio spans 16 properties across hotels, restaurants, banquet venues, and pilgrim residences. In India, 13 diverse establishments serve Ahmedabad — one of South Asia's most dynamic commercial cities. Internationally, we manage three distinguished pilgrim residences in the Holy Cities of Najaf, Kazmain, and Karbala — properties that have made us deeply familiar with the expectations of multi-cultural, multi-ethnic, and multi-faith guests from across the Arab world, South Asia, and beyond.

Oman's hospitality revenues reached a record OMR 297.3 million in 2025, up 22.2% year-on-year. The India–Oman CEPA signed December 2025 explicitly named hospitality as a priority sector for joint investment. The Ministry of Heritage and Tourism has signed 36 usufruct contracts valued at OMR 100 million for new tourism projects. The conditions are, in every meaningful sense, ideal.

We do not approach Oman as a new venture. We approach it as practitioners — with years of lived experience managing guests from across the world, serving those with deep religious and cultural sensitivities, and operating food and hospitality services that are 100% Halal, vegetarian-led, and internationally minded. We present this proposal with humility and respect, and we welcome any dialogue the Ministry may wish to initiate.

International Management Credentials

Proven in the World's Most
Demanding Environments

Our group manages three internationally recognised pilgrim residences in the Holy Cities of Iraq — operated on behalf of Anjuman e Faiz e Panjetani, a charitable trust founded in 1912. We handle every aspect of these operations except ownership.

These residences welcome guests arriving from Iran, India, Pakistan, the UK, Australia, the United States, East Africa, and across the Arab world — all with different languages, dietary requirements, cultural expectations, and religious sensitivities. Managing this complexity with warmth, consistency, and deep respect has sharpened a capability that very few Indian hospitality groups can claim: genuine multi-cultural, multi-ethnic, multi-faith hospitality at an international standard.

Our Proposal

What We Are
Formally Proposing

We present this proposal to the Ministry of Heritage and Tourism and relevant Omani authorities, offering our hospitality management capabilities, our international credentials, and our sincere interest in contributing to the Sultanate's Vision 2040 hospitality ambitions.

01

Proven International Management

We have managed three internationally positioned properties in Iraq's Holy Cities since 2016 — serving guests from 30+ nations. This is not theoretical capability; it is an operational track record.

02

Full Halal Operations Readiness

Every kitchen we run is Halal-compliant. We operate gender-sensitive spaces, accommodate prayer-time requirements, and serve both vegetarian and non-vegetarian Halal food — from Day One.

03

Complete Operations — Excluding Ownership

Just as we manage the Iraq properties without owning them, we propose to bring full operational capability to an Oman property under a management arrangement. The Omani partner owns; we operate with excellence.

04

Multi-Cuisine Kitchen Expertise

Deployment-ready menus spanning Indian, Continental, Levantine, Asian, and fusion cuisines — calibrated to serve the international visitor profile of Muscat International Airport.

05

Trained Hospitality Teams

Our Iraq operations have trained and managed front-of-house, kitchen, and housekeeping teams across cultures. We can deploy experienced personnel or train local Omani staff to our standards.

06

CEPA-Backed Legal Framework

The India–Oman CEPA provides a clear, Government-endorsed legal pathway for Indian hospitality companies to operate in Oman. We are ready to formalise a structure as soon as dialogue begins.

Proposed Arrangements

How We Might
Work Together

Management Contract

We operate and manage a new hospitality facility near Muscat International Airport. The Omani authority or investor owns the asset; we bring the full operating system, staff, and brand standards.

Joint Venture

Establish a jointly held entity under the CEPA FDI framework. We contribute operational expertise, established management systems, and culinary concepts. The Omani partner contributes location and regulatory facilitation.

Concept Licensing

License our established restaurant concepts — Jungle Bhookh, La Serata, Epitome, The Primo — for operation by an Omani licensee with our full brand, recipe, and operational support.

Advisory & Technical

Provide structured consulting: staff training, menu development, hospitality systems, kitchen setup, and quality standards — for an Omani operator seeking to raise the bar.

Priority Interest

Muscat International Airport Corridor

We are specifically interested in restaurant, café, and hotel management opportunities within the Muscat Airport zone and any upcoming integrated hospitality development in its vicinity. With 13.2 million annual passengers — and the government's expression of interest for the old terminal redevelopment — this is the single highest-potential location for our capabilities.
