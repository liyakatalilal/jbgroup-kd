Executive Summary:
This document presents a comprehensive research brief supporting a pitch by the Epitome–Volga Group — a multi-brand hospitality conglomerate headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India — to partner with Oman's Ministry of Heritage and Tourism (MHT) and relevant investment authorities for the development of hotel and restaurant properties near Muscat International Airport and across the Sultanate.

The Group operates 13 distinct brands across fine dining, themed restaurants, boutique hotels, banquet halls, catering, and multi-cuisine outlets. With deep operational roots in Ahmedabad — one of India's fastest-growing hospitality cities — the Group brings a proven track record of high-quality, experience-driven hospitality across price tiers, from budget-smart to premium.
